# PMI PMBOK Framework Blueprint

## What It Is
The Project Management Institute (PMI) standard covering Process Groups (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, Closing) and 10 Knowledge Areas.

## 10 Knowledge Areas
Integration, Scope, Schedule, Cost, Quality, Resource, Communications, Risk, Procurement, Stakeholder Management.

## When to Use
- Enterprise-level project portfolio management and budgeting.
