# PRINCE2 Project Governance Blueprint

## What It Is
PRINCE2 (PRojects IN Controlled Environments) is a process-based method for effective project management emphasizing business justification and defined organization structure.

## Core Elements
- **7 Principles**: Continued business justification, learn from experience, defined roles, manage by stages, manage by exception, focus on products, tailor to environment.
- **Project Board**: Executive (Business), Senior User, Senior Supplier.

## When to Use
- High-level governance of large multi-vendor transformation programs.
