# Roadmap Stage 00: Organization & Business Context

## Purpose & Scope
Before writing software or adopting enterprise frameworks, the engineering organization must understand Arian Khodro's business landscape: its commercial model, revenue drivers, dealership networks, and customer touchpoints.

## Key Architectural Dimensions

### 1. Business Model & Value Streams
- **Vehicle Import & Distribution**: Handling automotive import logistics, customs, assembly, dealership allocation, and showroom sales.
- **After-Sales Services & Maintenance**: Workshop repair scheduling, warranty processing, technician task management, customer reception.
- **Spare Parts Supply Chain**: Central warehousing, dealership inventory replenishment, supplier replenishment, catalog management.
- **Financing & Customer Lifecycle**: Payment plans, installment tracking, customer retention, loyalty programs.

### 2. Business Capabilities
Capabilities define *what* the business does independently of *how* or *who*. Key core domains:
- Commercial & Vehicle Sales Management
- Spare Parts Inventory & Fulfillment
- Service Workshop & Maintenance Operations
- Accounting, Finance & Payment Processing
- Customer Relationship & Vehicle Registry

### 3. Stakeholder Ecosystem
- **Executive Leadership**: Governance, ROI, strategic capability independence.
- **Dealership & Workshop Managers**: High daily operational throughput, low latency UI, offline resilience.
- **Technical & Engineering Teams**: Clear architecture boundaries, maintainability, modern CI/CD.
- **End Customers**: Transparent service booking, vehicle tracking, mobile interfaces.

## AI in this Stage

### AI Assistance
- **Industry & Domain Benchmarking**: Synthesize automotive distribution, dealership network models, and supply chain best practices.
- **Document Synthesis**: Parse organizational charts, operational memos, and legacy documentation into initial capability drafts.
- **Discovery Interview Preparation**: Formulate tailored stakeholder inquiry questionnaires based on initial domain hypotheses.

### Human Decision
- **Commercial Validation**: Confirming revenue drivers, business model boundaries, and operational priorities.
- **Strategic Direction**: Approving which business capabilities are core competitive differentiators vs utility functions.

### Human Approval
- **Required Sign-Off**: Executive Leadership & Head of Enterprise Architecture.

### Risks
- **Context Hallucination**: AI assuming generic automotive dealership models that contradict Arian Khodro's actual supply and customs realities.
- **Unverified Assumptions**: Mistaking hypothetical operational workflows for actual business practices.

### Required Evidence
- Signed-off Business Model Canvas.
- Validated Capability Catalog & Organizational Boundaries document.
- Documented Stakeholder Discovery Interview Transcripts.
