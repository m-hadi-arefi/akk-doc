# Transformation Leader Charter

## Executive Summary
The Transformation Leader is not merely a Chief Technology Officer or an enterprise architect; this role is the operational owner of the **Enterprise Transformation Operating System**. This charter defines the mission, scope of authority, decision rights, governance boundaries, relationships with executive stakeholders, and required 90-day and 12-month outcomes for the incoming transformation executive at Arian Khodro.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        TRANSFORMATION LEADER GOVERNANCE MANDATE                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  MISSION:      Unify fragmented operations into a data-driven, agile automotive engine.│
│  ACCOUNTABLE:  Directly to the Chief Executive Officer & Board Steering Committee.    │
│  CORE SPAN:    Business Process Modernization + Software Capability + Governed AI.     │
│  AUTHORITY:    Prune low-value initiatives, mandate engineering standards, set rules.  │
│  BOUNDARY:     Does NOT manage daily operational departments (Finance, Sales, etc.).  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 1. Role Definition & Strategic Scope

### Mission Statement
To lead, design, and govern the transition of Arian Khodro from a manual, siloed importer into an agile, transparent automotive leader by modernizing core business processes, instilling in-house software capabilities, and deploying pragmatic, secure automation.

### Scope of Authority
1. **Enterprise Architecture & Standards:** Absolute authority over technology selection, database patterns, API designs, and integration contracts.
2. **Portfolio Governance:** Proposed authority to cancel, freeze, or redirect software projects that lack verified business outcomes.
3. **Sourcing Decisions:** Authority to decide whether a required business capability is Built, Bought, Integrated, or Outsourced based on the formal decision framework.
4. **Data Governance & Integrity:** Authority to define data stewardship rules, eliminating departmental spreadsheet silos.

## 3. What the Transformation Leader Owns vs. Does NOT Own

### What This Person OWNS:
- The Enterprise Transformation Architecture and target operating model.
- The Requirements Traceability Matrix connecting business pain to software features.
- In-house engineering culture, technical hiring bars, and software delivery standards.
- Data governance policies and Master Data (VIN / Parts) definitions.
- The AI Governance Framework ensuring safe, human-in-the-loop deployments.

### What This Person DOES NOT Own:
- Daily operational departmental management (e.g., executing customs declarations, selling cars, managing warehouse inventory).
- Corporate financial budgeting and treasury operations.
- Employment decisions regarding existing line-of-business departmental employees.

---

## 4. Key Relationships & Operating Cadence

### Relationship with the CEO
- **Direct Strategic Advisory:** Bi-weekly 1-on-1 strategic reviews; no administrative bureaucracy.
- **Escalation Arbiter:** When departmental resistance impedes enterprise data sharing, the CEO acts as executive sponsor to break deadlocks based on the Leader's recommendations.

### Relationship with Business Department Heads
- **Collaborative Problem Solver:** The Leader does not impose ivory-tower solutions; every initiative begins by listening to departmental pain points during Discovery.
- **Process Optimization Partner:** Jointly redesigns broken handoffs before applying software automation.

### Relationship with IT & Engineering
- **Engineering Mentor & Culture Catalyst:** Instills modern software engineering practices (automated testing, code review, CI/CD, trunk-based development) and elevates talent.

### Relationship with External Vendors
- **Strict Accountability Enforcer:** Eliminates vendor lock-in by enforcing mandatory open APIs, 100% source code ownership, and rigorous acceptance testing.

## 5. First 90 Days Objectives & 12-Month Expected Outcomes

### First 90 Days Deliverables:
1. Complete empirical Discovery across Business, Technology, Data, People, and AI Readiness.
2. Publish the verified Pain Points & Hypotheses Registry with quantified baselines.
3. Deploy the read-only Tadark integration adapter safely into staging.
4. Deliver one end-to-end operational pilot (e.g., Customs VIN Ingestion Workflow).

### 12-Month Outcomes:
1. Live Core Platform running VIN Master Data with zero disruption to daily Tadark accounting.
2. Complete elimination of multi-departmental spreadsheet dependencies for vehicle import reconciliation.
3. Operational partner API deployed for authorized dealership spare parts tracking.
4. In-house engineering squad delivering production releases every two weeks.
