# Organization, Culture & Capability Assessment

## Executive Summary
Technology transformations fail primarily due to organizational and cultural misalignment, not software bugs. In Arian Khodro, departments have operated in protected functional silos for years, relying on personal relationships, informal agreements, and individual spreadsheets. This document outlines how the incoming Transformation Leader evaluates digital skills, maps change readiness and resistance, addresses key-person risks, and structures a high-performance in-house engineering team.

## 1. Digital Skills & Capabilities Heatmap
- **Commercial & Logistics:** Proficient in desktop spreadsheet manipulation and customs portal filings. Strong operational intuition; low familiarity with automated workflow systems or data validation tools.
- **Finance & Accounting:** High domain mastery of Iranian statutory accounting rules and Tadark desktop modules. Heavy reliance on manual spreadsheet reconciliation between bank statements and sales ledgers.
- **Warehouse & Compound Logistics:** Skilled in physical vehicle inspection and storage management. Relies on paper clipboards; low familiarity with mobile barcoding or digital handheld scanners.
- **Internal IT Department:** Historical mandate restricted to desktop helpdesk, server hardware repairs, local LAN networking, and CCTV maintenance. Completely lacking software engineering, modern cloud architecture, automated QA, or product management capabilities.

---

## 2. Mapping Cultural Dynamics & Change Resistance
Resistance to digital transformation typically stems from three human concerns:
1. **Fear of Loss of Control:** Departmental managers who currently control private spreadsheets possess informational leverage. Standardizing data into an open platform feels like losing authority.
2. **Fear of Increased Scrutiny:** Transparent digital pipelines reveal process latencies, errors, and idle time that were previously hidden inside manual handoffs.
3. **Fatigue from Past Failed IT Projects:** Staff members who witnessed previous vendor software failures approach new transformation initiatives with deep cynicism.

---

## 3. The Human Transformation Strategy: Co-Creation & Psychological Safety
To turn resistance into active advocacy:
- **No Blame for Legacy Workarounds:** We treat spreadsheets as evidence of resourceful employees trying to solve problems despite missing enterprise tools.
- **Co-Design of Workflows:** Departmental power users will actively design the new system screens and approve workflow steps during Discovery.
- **Incentivizing Transparency:** Staff KPIs will reward timely data entry and process velocity rather than penalizing historical clerical errors.

---

## 4. Building the Core In-House Transformation Squad
Because existing IT staff specialize in infrastructure maintenance, Arian Khodro will recruit a focused, high-caliber cross-functional transformation squad:
- **Lead Software Architect (Principal):** Owns system design, modular monolith patterns, and API governance.
- **Senior Full-Stack Engineers (2):** Execute modern frontend (React/TypeScript) and backend (Node.js/Go/PostgreSQL) services.
- **Technical Product Manager (1):** Bridges business value streams to engineering sprint backlogs.
- **Business Systems Analyst (1):** Conducts process discovery, requirements tracing, and user acceptance testing.
- **Change Management & Training Lead (1):** Champions user adoption, writes clear documentation, and trains departmental staff.
