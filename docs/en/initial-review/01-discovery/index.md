# Discovery: Empirical Foundation of Transformation

## Executive Summary
Discovery is the foundational phase of Arian Khodro's transformation. We firmly reject entering the organization with pre-packaged assumptions or off-the-shelf software prescriptions. Instead, the incoming Transformation Leader conducts a rigorous, evidence-based assessment over the first 30 to 60 days to replace speculation with measured operational facts across Business, Technology, Data, People, and AI Readiness.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE 5 LENSES OF EMPIRICAL DISCOVERY                             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. BUSINESS      ► Map true process flows, handoff friction, and cycle times.         │
│  2. TECHNOLOGY    ► Audit legacy Tadark Delphi code, data structures, and tech debt.   │
│  3. DATA          ► Inventory VIN lifecycle, parts catalogs, spreadsheets, and silos.  │
│  4. PEOPLE        ► Assess skills, change resistance, key-person risk, and incentives. │
│  5. AI READINESS  ► Evaluate document structure, OCR feasibility, and governance gaps.│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## The Scientific Discovery Principle
Every assertion produced during Discovery must adhere to an audit chain:
**Question** ➔ **Evidence Collected** ➔ **Empirical Finding** ➔ **Strategic Decision** ➔ **Operational Action**

No software will be designed, purchased, or built until the corresponding operational problem is verified by physical evidence (audit logs, time-and-motion studies, error rates, or database schema dumps).
