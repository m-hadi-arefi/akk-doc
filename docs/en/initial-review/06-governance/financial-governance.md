# Financial Governance & ROI Tracking

## Executive Summary
Digital transformation is a capital investment that must generate demonstrable, auditable financial returns. At Arian Khodro, we reject vague promises of "digital maturity" as justification for capital expenditure. This document defines our **Financial Governance Framework**: establishing strict stage-gate budget releases, tracking squad burn rates, and calculating verified ROI through tangible operational cost avoidance (demurrage penalties, overtime labor, duplicate software licenses).

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        FINANCIAL ROI DRIVERS & VALUE EQUATION                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  DIRECT COST AVOIDANCE   ► Elimination of southern port container demurrage penalties  │
│  LABOR PRODUCTIVITY      ► Hundreds of hours redirected from data entry to analysis     │
│  COMMERCIAL ACCELERATION ► Faster vehicle compound turnover and retail allocation      │
│  SOFTWARE TCO EFFICIENCY ► In-house platform eliminates recurring proprietary licenses │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 1. The Stage-Gate Capital Release Model
Budget is not allocated as a blank-check multi-year lump sum. Capital is released incrementally:
- **Tranche 1 (Days 0 - 60):** Discovery & Proof-of-Concept envelope (Covers squad salaries, server infrastructure, initial assessment tooling).
- **Tranche 2 (Days 61 - 90):** Pilot 1 & Sourcing envelope (Released upon verified completion of discovery baseline findings).
- **Tranche 3 (Months 3 - 6):** Core Platform alpha build (Released upon proven ROI and operational adoption of Pilot 1).
- **Tranche 4 (Months 6 - 12):** Dealership expansion envelope (Released upon successful VIN Master Data stabilization).

---

## 2. ROI Calculation Methodology & Formula
The net financial return of transformation is audited quarterly using the formal equation:
$$\text{Net ROI} = \frac{\text{Demurrage Avoided} + \text{Labor Savings} + \text{Software Fees Eliminated} - \text{Squad Engineering TCO}}{\text{Squad Engineering TCO}} \times 100$$

### Verified Value Vector 1: Demurrage Penalty Avoidance
- Every week shaved off customs clearance directly avoids heavy shipping line demurrage fees at southern ports.
- Verified by cross-referencing shipping container return receipts against historical demurrage averages.

### Verified Value Vector 2: Reclaimed Administrative Labor
- Eliminating manual typing of VIN manifests saves an estimated 150+ professional person-hours monthly across Commercial and Logistics teams.
- Staff capacity is redirected into supplier price negotiations and logistics route optimization.

### Verified Value Vector 3: Elimination of Duplicate Vendor Licenses
- Building the modular in-house platform retires proposals from external commercial software vendors quoting massive annual maintenance and customization fees.

---

## 3. Financial Accountability & Transparency
- The Transformation Leader and CFO review monthly budget burn rates during the first week of every month.
- Variance exceeding 10% on any operational expenditure line item triggers an immediate mandatory review by the Steering Committee.
