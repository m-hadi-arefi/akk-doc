## 1. International Engineering & Architecture Standards

### The TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition (The Open Group)
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Provides the macro-lifecycle guiding our transformation: Preliminary Phase (Discovery), Phase A (Architecture Vision), Phase B (Business Architecture), and Phase C/D (Information Systems & Technology Architecture).

### BABOK Guide v3 (Business Analysis Body of Knowledge)
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Guides process mapping, value stream identification, and requirements elicitation during Phase 1 Discovery.

### ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 Systems & Software Engineering
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Mandates our clear classification of Stakeholders, Concerns, Views, and Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

### The Prosci ADKAR Model
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Governs people enablement and cultural change management: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement.

### ISO 3779:2009 Road Vehicles — Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Governs the mathematical structure, character constraints, and 9th-digit modulus 11 checksum algorithm utilized by our VIN validation engines.

### DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) Metrics
- **Application in Arian Khodro:** Telemetry metrics benchmark squad software delivery velocity: Deployment Frequency, Lead Time for Changes, Change Failure Rate, and Time to Restore Service.

## 2. Iranian Statutory & Regulatory Frameworks
- **Comprehensive Trade System (سامانه جامع تجارت):** Regulates proforma registration, import licenses, and commercial currency quota allocations.
- **National Taxpayer System (سامانه مودیان مالیاتی):** Governs statutory e-invoicing, digital signature verification, and VAT compliance rules.
- **Islamic Republic of Iran Customs Administration (IRICA - گمرک ج.ا.ا):** Mandates electronic green leaf declarations, warehouse intake slips, and tariff HS code classifications.

- **Open Questions:** Tracking updates to the national Taxpayer System digital certificate signing libraries.
