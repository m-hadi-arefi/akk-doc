# Change Management & Organizational Enablement

## Executive Summary
The graveyard of enterprise software is filled with technically competent systems that were rejected by their human users. At Arian Khodro, change management is treated as an engineering discipline equal in priority to software development. Grounded in the **Prosci ADKAR Model**, this document establishes our structured strategy for building awareness, cultivating desire, imparting digital knowledge, fostering ability, and reinforcing continuous adoption across all departments.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE PROSCI ADKAR CHANGE JOURNEY                                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  AWARENESS   ► Explain WHY legacy tools threaten company survival (Honest town halls)  │
│  DESIRE      ► Show WHAT IS IN IT FOR ME (Eliminating mundane typing and overtime)    │
│  KNOWLEDGE   ► Hands-on, empathy-led training sessions (No dry 200-page user manuals)  │
│  ABILITY     ► Super-user side-by-side coaching on real live shipments and dossiers    │
│  REINFORCE   ► Celebrate quick wins, reward transparency, track adoption telemetry     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 1. The ADKAR Enablement Journey

### Step 1: Awareness (Building the Common Why)
- The CEO and Transformation Leader lead all-hands briefings explaining that modern systems are essential to protect the company against supply chain disruption, demurrage penalties, and regulatory fines.
- Explicit reassurance: Automation aims to eliminate drudgery, not eliminate jobs.

### Step 2: Desire (WIIFM — What's In It For Me?)
- For warehouse workers: No more freezing on clipboards in winter; lightweight handheld scanners with large touch buttons.
- For commercial specialists: No more spending weekends typing 17-character VINs from PDF manifests into Tadark.
- For accountants: Real-time reconciled balance sheets; no more stressful month-end discrepancy scrambles.

### Step 3: Knowledge (Interactive, Role-Based Training)
- Short, 15-minute role-based video walkthroughs and interactive sandbox practice environments.
- Zero jargon: instructions use familiar automotive terminology instead of abstract technical phrases.

### Step 4: Ability (Coaching & The Super-User Network)
- Embed "Super-Users"—respected frontline power users from Commercial, Logistics, and Warehouse—directly into the squads.
- Super-users provide immediate peer-to-peer desk-side assistance when colleagues encounter questions.

### Step 5: Reinforcement (Rewarding Adoption & Closing Loops)
- Feature adoption metrics are tracked weekly (percentage of dossiers processed digitally vs. manually).
- Departments achieving the fastest digital transition are publicly recognized and awarded performance bonuses in monthly company all-hands meetings.

## 2. Managing Resistance & Psychological Safety
Resistance is natural when established routines change. Our operating rules:
- **Never Shame Legacy Workarounds:** Spreadsheets are celebrated as past ingenuity.
- **Listen Before Defending:** If a user complains a new screen is slower than their old spreadsheet, engineers immediately observe them and optimize the keyboard flow.
- **Immediate Bug Escalation:** User-reported bugs during the first 30 days receive same-day engineering triage.
