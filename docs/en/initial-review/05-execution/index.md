# Execution, Delivery & Change Management

A world-class transformation strategy is worthless without disciplined operational execution. This section establishes the delivery engine of Arian Khodro: our bi-weekly dual-track agile cadence, tactical quick-win pilots, the Prosci ADKAR change management framework, and our comprehensive enterprise risk mitigation matrix.
