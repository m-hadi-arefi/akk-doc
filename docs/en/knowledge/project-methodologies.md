# Software Project Delivery Methodologies

Selecting the appropriate project management methodology depends on requirement stability, technical uncertainty, and team capacity. This document evaluates delivery models and confirms the operational framework for Arian Khodro.

---

## 1. Methodology Comparison

| Methodology | Guiding Philosophy | Key Strengths | Main Challenges | Fit for Arian Khodro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scrum** | Agile, iterative 2–4 week sprints | Rapid stakeholder feedback, adaptive to changing market conditions | Requires disciplined collaboration and transparent communication | **Very High** (Primary delivery framework for modern platform) |
| **Kanban** | Continuous workflow management | Excellent for bug triage, ad-hoc support, and continuous deployment | Lacks fixed time-boxed sprint commitments | **Moderate** (Ideal for legacy maintenance streams) |
| **Rational Unified Process (RUP)** | Architecture-centric, 4 phased stages | Strong risk management and comprehensive documentation | Heavy administrative overhead and slower initial velocity | **Low** (Theoretical concepts referenced only) |
| **Waterfall** | Sequential phase-gate execution | Predictable upfront budgeting and contract management | High risk of delivering software that no longer meets business needs | **Unsuitable** for dynamic transformation |

---

## 2. Selected Delivery Framework: Hybrid Agile

- **Core Delivery**: 2-week sprints delivering working software increments directly reviewed by executive leadership.
- **Architecture Governance Gates**: Strict Lead Architect sign-offs before merging cross-cutting foundational services.
