# Architecture & Engineering Frameworks

To guarantee rigor, avoid reinventing standard methodologies, and adopt global engineering best practices, Arian Khodro aligns its digital transformation with established industry standards.

---

## 1. Enterprise Architecture: TOGAF Standard

- The TOGAF Architecture Development Method (ADM) structures our enterprise views into four domains:
  - **Business Architecture**: Business strategy, organization governance, and key automotive processes.
  - **Data Architecture**: Master data entities, logical schemas, and data pipelines.
  - **Application Architecture**: Application portfolios, APIs, and service boundaries.
  - **Technology Architecture**: Cloud infrastructure, network topology, and security controls.

---

## 2. Business Analysis: BABOK Guide

- The BABOK Body of Knowledge governs stakeholder engagement and solution verification:
  - Clear delineation between Business Requirements, Stakeholder Requirements, and Technical Specifications.
  - Value stream mapping and return on investment (ROI) validation before code production.

---

## 3. Process Modeling: BPMN 2.0

- International graphical notation standard for unambiguous workflow representation across procurement, customs, logistics, and after-sales service.

---

## 4. Architecture Description: ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010

- Formal architecture documentation aligned with explicit stakeholder concerns (CEO, finance, workshop managers, and vehicle owners).
