# Step 1: AI Training for Executives & Staff Enablement

The primary determinant of enterprise AI adoption is human alignment and leadership understanding of actual technological capabilities versus limitations. In this first step of the Arian Khodro AI strategy, the focus centers on pragmatic training, dispelling organizational apprehension, and enabling executive and departmental managers.

---

## 1. Executive Curriculum & Hands-On Workshops

Workshops deliberately bypass theoretical mathematics to concentrate exclusively on solving automotive business problems:

1. **Pragmatic Enterprise AI: Realities vs Hype**
   - Dissecting real-world applications across automotive supply chains and separating marketing promises from tangible engineering value.
2. **Prompt Engineering in Commercial & Financial Operations**
   - Practical skills in extracting tabular figures from foreign proformas, drafting multilingual supplier communications, and summarizing regulatory customs bulletins.
3. **Executive Decision Validation & Scenario Modeling**
   - Leveraging generative and analytical models to model exchange rate sensitivities, analyze competitor offerings, and accelerate briefing materials for the CEO.

---

## 2. Overcoming Organizational Resistance

Many staff members harbor anxieties regarding automation. These enablement sessions demonstrate that:
- AI will not displace human domain experts; rather, **professionals leveraging AI will outperform those who do not.**
- AI removes tedious, repetitive spreadsheet data-entry tasks, freeing employees to concentrate on relationship management, strategic negotiation, and quality control.

---

## 3. Success Metrics for Step 1

| Performance Indicator | Target Threshold | Verification Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive & Departmental Attendance** | > 90% participation | Completion of practical workflow assignments |
| **Productivity in Drafting Reports** | ≥ 40% time reduction | Baseline measurement of briefing compilation time |
| **Positive Change Management Sentiment** | > 80% favorable feedback | Internal sentiment survey and workflow submission portal |
