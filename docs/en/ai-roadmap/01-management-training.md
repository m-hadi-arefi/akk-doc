# Step 1: Management Enablement & Practical AI Training

The primary failure mode in corporate AI initiatives is employee inertia and leadership misunderstandings of model capabilities. Step 1 focuses on upskilling leaders and key department heads with hands-on, domain-specific AI fluency.

---

## 1. Executive Workshop Curricula

- **What AI Is and What It Is Not**: Stripping away marketing hype to focus strictly on text extraction, summarization, entity recognition, and data synthesis in automotive trade.
- **Applied Prompt Engineering for Commercial Teams**: Formulating precise prompts to extract technical parts specifications, parse overseas supplier correspondences, and monitor competitor pricing.
- **Contract Summarization & Risk Screening**: Leveraging language models to flag penalty clauses and warranty exclusions in dealership and supplier agreements.

---

## 2. Key Performance Indicators for Step 1

| Metric | Target | Measurement Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive & Director Workshop Completion** | > 90% | Practical evaluation & workflow integration |
| **Commercial Drafting Time Reduction** | Minimum 40% reduction | Time-to-dispatch for supplier negotiations |
| **Organizational Sentiment Towards Automation** | > 80% positive feedback | Quarterly employee pulse surveys |
