# Step 3: Departmental Rollout of Production AI Tools

Step 3 operationalizes proven AI models directly into commercial, procurement, and after-sales workflows across Arian Khodro.

---

## 1. Tool 1: Customs Documentation & Shipping Bill OCR

- **Function**: Ingests PDF scans and camera captures of customs documents, automatically extracting:
  - Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) and engine numbers
  - Vehicle model, year of manufacture, and gross weight
  - Tariff codes, declared value, and duties payable
- **Business Impact**: Reduces data entry duration from 40 minutes per container shipment to under 2 minutes with near-zero clerical errors.

---

## 2. Tool 2: Automated 3-Way Invoice Matching Engine

```text
[Foreign Purchase Order (PO)] ──┐
                                ▼
[Warehouse Receipt (GRN)]     ──► [Automated Matching Engine] ──► [Auto-Approval / Anomaly Flag]
                                ▲
[Supplier Invoice / Tadark]   ──┘
```

- Automated reconciliation of part numbers, quantities, and unit pricing across purchase orders, warehouse receipts, and vendor invoices.
- In case of discrepancy, the system highlights the exact delta and routes the file to a senior commercial specialist for review.

---

## 3. Tool 3: Intelligent Commercial Pricing Assistant

- Evaluates historical import price points and real-time exchange rates.
- Recommends competitive retail and wholesale spare parts pricing to protect corporate profit margins against market volatility.
