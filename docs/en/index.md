## Executive Overview: The Three Core Principles

This knowledge portal establishes the strategic, architectural, and governance foundation for the modernization of **Arian Khodro**. The entire transformation is governed by three fundamental principles:

1. **Enterprise Standards & Pragmatic Governance:** We apply globally recognized methodologies (TOGAF, BABOK, BPMN 2.0, ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010) not as theoretical overhead, but as practical tools to prevent premature capital misallocation and costly architectural rework.
2. **Empathy for Operational Reality:** Modernization must address Arian Khodro's real daily challenges—legacy Tadark constraints, disconnected spreadsheets, physical paper latencies, data fragmentation, and reliance on key personnel—rather than chasing technological novelty.
3. **Disciplined, Phased Execution:** We define what must be done, in what exact sequence, why it must be done, **what must NOT be done yet**, and how risks are managed through formal Decision Gates.
