# Enterprise Standards Catalog

Every standard in this catalog is evaluated for its practical value to Arian Khodro.

---

## Standard: ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022
- **Purpose**: Systems and software engineering — Architecture description.
- **Scope**: Establishes a formal metamodel for framing concerns, defining stakeholders, constructing viewpoints, and recording architecture decisions.
- **When to Use**: All system architecture documentation and enterprise blueprints across Arian Khodro.
- **Priority**: Mandatory.

---

## Standard: BABOK v3 (Business Analysis Body of Knowledge)
- **Purpose**: Global standard for business analysis practices developed by IIBA.
- **Scope**: Strategy analysis, requirements elicitation, requirements analysis, solution evaluation.
- **When to Use**: Stage 01 of every software initiative before architecture design begins.
- **Priority**: Mandatory.

---

## Standard: TOGAF 10 & ArchiMate 3.2
- **Purpose**: Enterprise architecture methodology and visual modeling language.
- **Scope**: Business, Data, Application, and Technology (BDAT) alignment.
- **When to Use**: Enterprise architecture mapping, platform scope definitions.
- **Priority**: High.

---

## Standard: BPMN 2.0 (Business Process Model and Notation)
- **Purpose**: Graphical representation for specifying business processes in a workflow.
- **Scope**: AS-IS and TO-BE business process modeling across all departments.
- **When to Use**: Stage 02 of all digital transformation workflows.
- **Priority**: Mandatory.
