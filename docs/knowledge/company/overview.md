# Company & Business Overview

## About Arian Khodro
Arian Khodro is a prominent automotive enterprise engaged in importing, assembly, distribution, retail dealership sales, after-sales maintenance, and financing solutions.

## Business Domains
1. **Commercial Vehicle Sales**: Dealership allocation, customer pre-orders, delivery logistics, registration.
2. **After-Sales Services**: Dealership repair workshops, warranty inspections, maintenance scheduling.
3. **Spare Parts Supply Chain**: Central distribution center, regional hubs, authorized dealership inventory.
4. **Financial Services**: Customer installment financing, payment tracking, accounting, reconciliation.

## Operational Realities & Constraints
- **Legacy Systems**: Core historical accounting and parts inventory operated on Tadark (Delphi 32-bit).
- **Decoupled Modernization**: Transformation follows an incremental strangler strategy rather than risky big-bang replacement.
- **Strict Compliance**: Financial auditing, tax compliance, and vehicle safety registration regulations must be upheld continuously.
